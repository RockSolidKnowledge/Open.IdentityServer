# Open.IdentityServer Supporters ❤️

Open.IdentityServer is an open source project and is free for everyone to use.

We'd like to recognise the organisations and individuals who help make its continued development possible.

Supporters include those who have purchased a Supporter Licence, as well as organisations and individuals who have demonstrated significant support for Open.IdentityServer and the wider community.

## Become a Supporter

If your organisation relies on Open.IdentityServer, you can help support its continued development by purchasing a Supporter Licence.

The Supporter Licence provides access to the IdentityServer.com product portfolio while directly supporting the ongoing development of Open.IdentityServer.

[Learn more about supporting Open.IdentityServer](https://www.identityserver.com/)
